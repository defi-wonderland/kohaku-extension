# Sara, a guardian with a wallet of her own

Sara is Carl's friend and the one who got him into crypto. She is technical, runs her own wallet with an ordinary key she controls, and has approved things for friends before. She is not a holder in this design: she never sets up recovery here and never recovers an account. She is the guardian fixture, the person on the other end of a guardian row.

She holds one thing the design touches: the key Carl named as his guardian. She stands to lose nothing of her own beyond the publication of her address if a recovery ever runs, which Carl read at setup and asked her about first. Approving also links the accounts this key serves, which the page tells her. She stands to be the one who signs a phisher's key if she signs without calling Carl.

## Journey, approval

Carl calls Sara, as his row told him to, and reads her the account and the key that should take control. He sends her the approval page's link over the channel they already use, and she pastes it into a browser that has Kohaku, which she installs from the source that message names, checking the publisher it tells her to check (E1-01).

She reads the account, the new key in full, the key being removed and the words no payment, opens verify the details for the deadline in her own zone, the setup number, the attempt number, the chain as a fixed label and the manager deployment the request names. She reads the instruction to compare the account and the new key with what Carl read on the call and the line that approving puts her address on chain in the clear for good.

She compares the account and the new key, ticks that she called the owner on a number she already had and they confirmed this, connects the wallet installed in that browser, the route D-314 tests on the first day, and signs (E1-04, E1-05). She then sends the one-line approval back as the page tells her to. The page shows her address with its blockie, or a name a public service resolves for it, and never a name Carl typed.

## Journey, decline

A text arrives naming a recovery on Carl's account, with a key Carl never told her about. Sara calls Carl, who knows nothing of it. She signs nothing and installs nothing on the strength of a link she distrusts. She declines by telling Carl directly on the channel they already use.

The page's decline action, which asks the guardian to tell the owner anyway, is there for a guardian who already opened the page (E1-06). Carl, still holding his key, cancels the attempt from his own machine if it ever lands, and Sara's decline is what made him look.
