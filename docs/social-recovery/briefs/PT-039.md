## Outcome

`src/web/modules/social-recovery/shared/writes/` holds the submitting and failed states every write of the chapter shares and the gas check with its deposit step. Every owner-signed write and both recovery calls inherit these states, and a failed state that misreads a revert as a call never sent has the holder retry a call that cannot land.

## Design sections and deltas

- `design/ux.md` D-303 (the fast track's funding step: the account cannot pay for itself until it is recovered; the execution is a second funding), D-307 (the reverted cancel's reading: the attempt is already gone, with the account's controller as it now stands), D-312 (no faucet link on a test network; the first release configures no sponsor), D-319 (the two readings of the failed state), D-393 (the second funding at execution due at the fee of that day).
- `design/ux-interfaces.md` D-373 (the SDK estimates nothing; the extension's provider estimates and reads the balance).

## Interfaces and invariants

- Consumes PT-038's provider (balance and gas estimate), PT-038's signer facade (which key sends), and PT-036's chips.
- Invariants: none listed. The done entries: the submitting state and the failed state exist once and every write of the chapter renders them, the failed state carrying its two readings (nothing reached the chain and the account stands as it did; or the call reverted with the cause the receipt carries and its gas gone); the gas step estimates the transaction's own gas through the extension's provider, names the sending key's address, the amount and the network, offers a transfer from another account this wallet holds and a deposit from outside, and says a transfer out of the account that key operates is itself an operation that key must pay for; the step states that the execution after the waiting period is a second funding asked for again at the fee of that day and promises nowhere that one funding covers both, and it renders no faucet link.

## Shape

- A state machine as pure functions and types: `idle`, `checkingGas`, `needsDeposit`, `submitting`, `landed`, `failedNotSent`, `failedReverted` (names are the implementer's; the two failed readings are distinct states, never one state with a flag). The classification of a failure takes the error or receipt and decides "never sent" from "reverted" by whether a transaction hash exists and a receipt with status zero came back; the reverted cancel has its own reading per D-307.
- The gas check: a function taking the prepared call, the sending key and the provider reads, returning either "enough" or the deposit step's data (key address, estimated amount, network name, the two routes). Skipped when the key holds enough.
- Two React components render the states and the deposit step from that data through `socialRecovery.writes` keys; the rules live in the functions.
