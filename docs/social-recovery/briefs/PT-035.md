## Interfaces and invariants

- Implements: `ISetupClient`, `IRecoveryClient`, `IMethodsOrchestrator`, `IEventManager`, `IPolicyManagerInteractor`, `IMethodModuleReads`, `IProvider`, `IRecoveryActionInteractor`, `IRecoveryActionArming`, `IRecoveryMethod` (one scripted double per shipped kind: wallet, passkey, zkPassport, Aadhaar), `IActionCodec` and the builder of D-208. All types come from `@web/modules/social-recovery/sdk-interfaces`; the lane declares no interface of its own except the cut-q-22 seam.
- Invariants: none listed. The task's judgments stand in for them: every member exists with the records the sections fix; the scripted chain holds every state D-371 and D-373 name (setup none or committed under each of the three privacy levels; attempt none, pending, ready, cancelled by the account, by a caller with proofs or by nobody, or executed; authorization held or removed; code present or absent; one declaration per method with `trustedParties`, `paused`, `moduleInfo`; the manager's `stateOf`, `setupCommittedAtBlock`, `eip712Domain`; the action's `supportsAccount`, `isAuthority`, `holdsAnyPrivilege`); every read can be scripted to fail by throwing and every prepare to refuse with a code, and a failed read is a thrown value a screen can tell from an empty result.
- The event manager double serves `AttemptStarted`, `AttemptCancelled`, `AttemptConsumed`, `SetupCommitted` and `TrustedKeysUpdated` from the record. The recovery client double runs `initRecoveryGathering`, `getApproverRequests`, `addApproverReply` with its refusals as D-207 states them, and `complete` over the requests it minted. The orchestrator double answers `describeRequest`, `signingInput` and `replyFrom` from a request alone.
