## Outcome

`src/web/modules/social-recovery/shared/rule-lines/` holds one pure function that turns a path's shape into the rule lines D-305 states, in D-305's order. The wallet renders these lines in five places from this one function: the recommended-path card, the setup and management editors, the review, the recoverer's readout and the done screen's exits. The SDK returns no verdict on a rule; the lines are the wallet's own calls on rule quality, and a wrong line tells a holder a lockout is a rescue.

## Design sections and deltas

- `design/ux.md` D-305 (path building: every rule line and the shape that earns it) and D-312 (the decision record: one failure domain keyed on the method family, so a passport and an Aadhaar identity read as two domains; the identity method's weight line and the words "primary" and "offered" are produced by nothing).
- `design/ux-copy.md`: the bans, and UXC-10's concept names ("recovery path", "method").
- Deltas: none found in the task file. Strings live in `en.json` under `socialRecovery.ruleLines`, registered by the setup task with `{{n}}`, `{{m}}` and `{{spare}}` (M minus N) placeholders; this lane consumes keys and adds none. A missing line is reported to the coordinator, who registered four more forms at `c40d5dabb` (`togetherWithGroups`, `togetherWithRequiredEveryMember`, `togetherWithGroupsEveryMember`, `togetherWithRequiredAndGroupsEveryMember`).
- Coordinator's ruling (2026-09-24): D-305's sentence for a group whose threshold equals its member count is ambiguous between replacing and adding to the count line; frame C-04e renders a 3-of-3 group with only "Every member must answer.", so the every-member line replaces the count line. The together-with clause still applies to such a group through the every-member together-with forms. A threshold-one group beside rows or groups renders the together-with any-N-of-M wording with n=1. A path with any refused clause (an empty clause, a threshold of zero or below since the editor refuses a group nothing has to fill, a threshold above the member count, a non-integer threshold, a threshold above 255) or with one method enrolled twice across the path (D-305: one enrolled method appears once) yields no lines, since the lines describe the whole path and a refused path recovers nothing.
- For the owner: the chapter's own clause "one member of each other group" is false when another group's threshold is above one; the wording is D-305's and the keys copy it.

## Interfaces and invariants

- Input: the path record the setup draft holds, which is the setup draft record `sdk-interfaces/` declares from `sdk.md` D-202 (`validateSetup`, `describeSetup` and `prepareCommitSetup` read it): required rows and groups with their thresholds and members with their kinds. This lane imports that type and declares no path type of its own, so PT-040 (which stores the draft) and this lane meet on the setup's type without depending on each other.
- Output: the ordered list of lines, each as `{ key, params }` where `key` is an `en.json` key under `socialRecovery.ruleLines` and `params` carries `n`, `m` and any name the line needs, plus a `renderRuleLines(lines, t)` helper that resolves them. Tests assert both the descriptors and the rendered English.
- Invariants: none listed. The done entries are the rules: all N must answer (N required rows, losing any one locks you out); both must answer (two rows); the single-method warning (one row, or a group of one member) with the second passkey or hardware key offer beside it and the line that two passkeys behind one platform account share its fate; any N of these M recover this account, losing more than M minus N locks you out; either one alone can recover this account and either one alone can also take it (threshold one at two members); any one of these M alone (threshold one at more); together with your required methods and one member of each other group where required rows or a second group stand beside a group; every member must answer (threshold equals member count); one failure domain (all members share one method family, keyed on family per D-312); the setup line about keeping methods in different places; the sizing rule line for a two-item path as two required rows. The lines say nothing about the identity method's weight and nothing about raising a threshold when a secondary credential joins.
